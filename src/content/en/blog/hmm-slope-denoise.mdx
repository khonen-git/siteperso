<NotaBene>

**In brief.** I filtered OLS slope t-stat through a 3-state HMM (down / neutral / up) on horizons {16, 32, 64, 128}. **Soft-fade** episodes (short doubt vs long thesis) show stable structure: CIF **R/E/B ≈ 28/68/4%** IS≈OOS. **Mid-episode** risk is readable (AUC E ≈ **0.82**), not at onset (AUC ≈ **0.52**). Net EV ≈ **0** after costs.

</NotaBene>

## Why I ran this study

Raw slope is noisy; MAP alone is unstable bar-to-bar. Idea: causal **soft slopes** as a regime layer, then test whether a “fade” pattern (s16 turns neutral while s128 stays on thesis) carries tradable information.

Classic trap: confusing a high conditional realignment rate with a favourable onset probability.

## Setup (summary)

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|--------|
| Asset / barrier | EURUSD · TIMB |
| HMM fit | 60d 2023 → freeze |
| Eval | IS post-calib 2023 + **OOS 2024** |
| HMM | C2, K=3, sticky E[D]=40 |

Soft-fade onset (e.g. long): <MathInline>{"s_{16}"}</MathInline> drops from +1 to 0 while <MathInline>{"s_{128}=+1"}</MathInline>.

Absorbing outcomes: **Resolve** (R), **Escalate** (E), **Break** (B).

## Key results

PnL by outcome: resolve **~+4.3 pips**, escalate **~−1.8** — with ~**68%** escalate, the book dies.

## Takeaways

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Is the denoiser sound? | **Yes** — fast (~23 s/yr), stable freeze |
| Entry gate at onset? | **No** |
| Dynamic mid-episode risk? | **Yes** — person-period |
| Standalone trade? | **No** |

> “~90% realignment” is **conditional** on R∪B — not P(winning onset).

**Recommended use:** multi-scale softs as **regime / hazard features** — not an isolated entry signal.

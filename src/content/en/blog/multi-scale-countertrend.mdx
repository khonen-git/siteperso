<NotaBene>

**In brief.** When the short trend (DC 5p) diverges from the long one (DC 15p), the market enters a measurable “pullback” state. In 2023, **~79%** of episodes resolve as *Resume* (R), stable disc/dev. The causal score predicts R vs F **OOS** (ΔLL up to **+137**). Net tradability **fails**: EV **−0.22 pip** on dev under 0.2 spread.

</NotaBene>

## Why I ran this study

The most visual price-action motif: a background trend with a shorter counter-move. Before trading anything, I asked whether this is a **process** (R/F states with competing risks) or labelled noise.

The **gates 0→4** framework enforces: existence → detection → predictability → tradability — without tuning upstream gates on PnL.

## Definition (summary)

An episode starts when <MathInline>{"\\mathrm{sign}(T_L) \\neq \\mathrm{sign}(T_S)"}</MathInline> (long DC 15p vs short DC 5p), with minimal conflict of 3 bars. Outcomes:

| Outcome | Symbol | Meaning |
|---------|--------|---------|
| Resume | R | Short realigns with long |
| Fail | F | Long structure breaks |
| Censor | C | Series end / split |

<MathBlock>{"p(R \\mid R \\cup F) \\approx 0{,}79 \\quad \\text{(stable disc/dev)}"}</MathBlock>

**R and F are structure states, not PnL.**

## Takeaways

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Does the pullback exist? | **Yes** — beats null, stable R rate |
| Can we predict outcome OOS? | **Yes** — significant ΔLL |
| Trade it as-is? | **No** — unfavourable MFE/MAE asymmetry |

> Strong **state predictability** does not imply an **executable edge**.

**Recommended use:** regime feature / filter in a downstream stack (HMM, Tr8dr permission) — not a standalone signal.
